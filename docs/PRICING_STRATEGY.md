# 💰 Tabibi AI (طبيبي) — Commercial Pricing Strategy & Unit Economics

> **Target Market**: Egyptian Private Medical Clinics & Individual Doctors  
> **Currency**: Egyptian Pound (EGP)

---

## 1. Evaluation of Business Models

| Model | Business Impact | Risk / Reward | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A) One-Time Payment** | High initial cash flow, but zero recurring revenue to cover monthly OpenAI token & hosting costs. | **High Risk**: Client burns AI tokens for years without paying. | ❌ Not Recommended |
| **B) Pure Monthly Subscription** | Low barrier to entry, but high churn risk if doctor cancels after 1 month. | **Medium Risk**: Setup effort not paid upfront. | ❌ Not Recommended |
| **C) Setup Fee + Monthly Subscription** | Covers initial onboarding & WhatsApp setup costs while securing recurring revenue. | **Optimal**: High commitment, predictable cash flow. | ✅ **RECOMMENDED MODEL** |

## 2. Product Packages & Commercial Tiers

### Tier 1: Starter Package (باقة البداية للأطباء)
- **Target**: Single-branch doctor starting with WhatsApp automation.
- **Setup Fee**: **7,500 EGP** (one-time)
- **Monthly Subscription**: **1,500 EGP / month**
- **Included Features**:
  - 1 Doctor Profile & 1 Clinic Branch.
  - 24/7 AI WhatsApp Receptionist (Assistant: "مريم").
  - Deterministic Booking Flow & Cancellation.
  - 10 Custom FAQs & Services.
  - Daily Automated 24h Reminders.
  - Web Dashboard access for 1 Receptionist.

### Tier 2: Professional Package (باقة العيادة الاحترافية - Recommended Default)
- **Target**: Established doctor with high message volume or 2 branches.
- **Setup Fee**: **12,500 EGP** (one-time)
- **Monthly Subscription**: **2,500 EGP / month**
- **Included Features**:
  - Everything in Starter.
  - Multi-Branch Location Support (Up to 3 Branches).
  - Custom AI Assistant Persona & Response Tone.
  - Unlimited Custom FAQs & Knowledge Base Retrieval.
  - Google Calendar Live Two-Way Sync.
  - Token Cost Audit & Executive Analytics Dashboard.
  - Priority WhatsApp Support.

### Tier 3: Premium / Multi-Doctor Center Package (باقة المراكز الطبية)
- **Target**: Medical centers or doctor partners sharing a clinic.
- **Setup Fee**: **25,000 EGP** (one-time)
- **Monthly Subscription**: **4,500 EGP / month**
- **Included Features**:
  - Up to 3 Doctors included in one platform instance.
  - Custom API integrations & n8n workflow triggers.
  - Dedicated Account Manager & On-site Receptionist Training.
