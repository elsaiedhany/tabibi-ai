## 1. Egyptian Medical Software Market Landscape

### Market Competitors & Positioning:
1. **Vezeeta (فيزيتا)**: Main marketplace player for doctor discovery. Charges 20%-30% commission per booking or high listing fees (25,000–50,000+ EGP/year). *Pain Point*: Doctors feel Vezeeta owns their patient list.
2. **CliniDo / Yodawy**: Listing platforms. Limited automated WhatsApp receptionist capabilities.
3. **Generic WhatsApp Chatbot Builders (ManyChat / Wati / Landbot)**: Cost $49–$199 USD/month + Meta conversation fees. Require heavy setup effort, lack Arabic medical state machine, lack emergency symptom detection, and have zero doctor-centric booking logic.

### Tabibi AI Strategic Advantage:
- **Direct WhatsApp Ownership**: Sold directly to the doctor for their private WhatsApp number.
- **Egyptian Arabic Native Intelligence**: Responds in natural Egyptian dialect ("أهلاً بحضرتك", "الكشف بـ 500 ج.م").
- **Zero Booking Commission**: Fixed monthly subscription regardless of patient volume.

- **"خايف من الذكاء الاصطناعي يغلط"**: *"السيستم بيعتمد على قواعد مبرمجة صارمة للحجز والأسعار، والذكاء الاصطناعي بيستخدم فقط في فهم طريقة كلام المريض. ولو فيه طوارئ السيستم بيحوله للاستقبال فوراً!"*
